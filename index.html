Connect your YouTube channel

📘 One-time setup (about 10 minutes): get your Client ID
  1. Open console.cloud.google.com and create a project (e.g. “AutoUploader”).
  2. APIs & Services → Library: search YouTube Data API v3 and click Enable.
  3. OAuth consent screen (Google Auth Platform): choose External, enter an app name and your email. Under Audience → Test users, add the Google account that owns your channel.
  4. Clients → Create client: type Web application. Under Authorized JavaScript origins, add exactly:
  5. Copy the Client ID, paste it above, and click Connect. Google warns that the app isn't verified. It's your own app, so click Continue and allow access.
  6. ⚠ Important for public videos: YouTube keeps every video uploaded through an unaudited Google project private, and those videos stay locked. Submit the free “YouTube API Services – Audit and Quota Extension” form for your project (search that name). Until it's approved, test with 1–2 clips only.
